The short answer

If sunshine on the beach matters most, pick Sri Lanka. Its south coast is in dry season in January, the flight from Chennai is short, the entry permit is free and the food is the easiest without pork or beef. You give up nightlife, a real coaster and some certainty on hotel prices.

For the trip you described (classes, nightlife, meeting people, a coaster, somewhere new), Vietnam version 1A still edges it, and it has by far the deepest data behind it. Bali is the one to skip in January: it is Bali's wettest month and the budget only just fits.

Eating without pork or beef

Vietnam

  • Hardest of the three: many famous dishes and broths use pork or beef.
  • Safe: cơm gà, grilled seafood, phở gà, bún cá.
  • Say: Không thịt heo, không thịt bò

Sri Lanka

  • Easiest: rice and curry with chicken or fish is the everyday meal; kottu and hoppers come with chicken or egg.
  • Tamil is an official language, so you can ask in Tamil.
  • Sinhala: ūru mas epā, harak mas epā (no pork, no beef)

Bali

  • Bali is mostly Hindu, so pork (babi guling) is everywhere and beef is rare. Indonesia is mostly Muslim, so halal warungs are easy to find.
  • Safe: nasi campur ayam, sate ayam, ikan bakar, udang.
  • Say: tanpa babi, tanpa sapi (no pork, no beef)
