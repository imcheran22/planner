# Reddit v1 findings (21 threads, 1,313 comments) — working notes for the itinerary

Trip: Fri 22 Jan – Thu 28 Jan 2027 (6 nights). Non-veg, no beef/pork. 2 beach-view nights. Classes + nightlife. Budget ₹2L total.
Tet 2027 = 6 Feb (T1) → trip is ~2 weeks before; no closures, "preparation events" (T1).

## Route evidence
- 5–7 days = one region (T10 tourism worker); "everything 1 night is a no-go" (T1, honeymoon reply).
- North in Jan: Jan travellers "more rainy/overcast days", cooler (T6/T7); Hanoi air quality poor (T6, T7); north cool + drizzly in winter (T1).
- Central rainy season Oct–Dec (T1 advisor); late-Nov/Dec central warnings in T12, T20. Jan central not directly discussed → verify weather.
- => Central (Hoi An / Hue / Da Nang) fits; beach-view room also needs coast.

## Places
- Hoi An: split. +night/early morning, countryside (Tra Que bike, coconut boat, cooking class, lantern workshop, egg-coffee workshop, My Son). − crowded, pricey, "influencer fake", old town = half day–1 day. Consensus 2 nights max; stay outside by beach = "best of both worlds" (T17).
- Da Nang: split. + clean beach, local food/cafés, Son Tra, Marble Mountains ("one of the most incredible places", "unmissable"), be there Fri/Sat/Sun (T15). − high-rises, "Vegas for Russians", hard to find authentic food near My Khe → go downtown (T17).
- Hue: strongly positive ("nicest surprise" T6, T19 "great stop", T11 #2 fav). Imperial City $8 (T6). Day trip from DN "too long, not enough time" (T13). Fly in / stay over, train or car via Hai Van.
- Hai Van: private car with stops (T1); Hue→Hoi An car ~$70 for two with stops (T7); private driver Hue 5h $45+tip (T6). Train scenic (T10, T21).
- Ba Na Hills: most polarised — 12 of 26 mentions negative ("tourist trap", "expensive and crowded", "gardens spectacular"). Wife-wants-Golden-Bridge case (T2).
- My Son: "shadow of former glory after bombings, still impressive" (T3); ~1.5h each way (T21).
- Cham Islands: "overlooked, Asia 15 years ago" (T1) → check Jan sea conditions.
- Lang Co beach: family loved 3 beach days (T3).

## Food (diet flag)
- Praised: Hoi An chicken rice ("best of my life" T3), white rose dumplings (shrimp), seafood, egg/salt/coconut coffee.
- Many famous central dishes are pork/beef: cao lau, bun bo Hue, nem lui, banh mi (usually pork), beef in betel leaf → plan chicken/seafood alternatives.
- Food tours ~$35, "a lot of food, great value" (T6). Local set menu Hue ~$6/person (T6).

## Practical
- Taxi scam: Grab 50k vs meter 800k (T3) → Grab / Xanh SM.
- Don't take restaurant recs from taxi drivers (commission) – ask hotel staff/waiters (T11).
- Hotels WhatsApp you with tours/transfers, often cheaper than online (T6, T7).
- Some properties demand cash even when booked online; Agoda bookings charged to card (T11).
- Count change at night markets (T14); vendors approach café tables (T6).
- Vietjet domestic non-refundable (T12).
- E-visa: ~10 working days before; list a few extra days (T11).
- Data eSIM + Google Translate fine (T6).
- Cities not walkable → Grab (T3, T6).
- Hotels $35–70/night mid-range; tours $24–35pp (T6).
- Hoi An/Ninh Binh quiet at night; Da Nang/Hanoi livelier (T21).
