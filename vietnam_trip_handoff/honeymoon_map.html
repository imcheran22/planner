    Things we both should know

    Eating: no pork, no beef

    Không thịt heo, không thịt bòNo pork, no beef
    Chỉ gà hoặc hải sảnOnly chicken or seafood
    • Safe: cơm gà (chicken rice), grilled seafood, white rose dumplings, phở gà, bún cá, chicken bánh mì without pâté.
    • Ask first: cao lầu, nem lụi, bánh xèo, bún bò Huế, phở bò. Many broths use pork bone.
    • Indian or halal backup: Kababish (4.9, 525), India0Miles (4.9, 253), Dhaba 21 Hoi An (5.0, 572), Spice Route (4.9, 1,048).

    Ba Na Hills: go or skip

    • Ticket 1,000,000 VND each. Coaster extra: 100,000 a ride or 250,000 for 3.
    • Top is about 15 °C. Bring a fleece and a rain jacket.
    • Clouds stick for the whole week in about half of years. Check the summit forecast 2–3 days before; if it's cloud all week, skip it and keep ₹12,076.
    • Fog tends to be thickest before 10:00 and builds again after about 14:00 (hoiandaytrip.com). Reddit says go at opening for the queues. Aim to be up top by 10:00 and ride the coaster first.
    • The coaster closes in rain or fog.

    Skip these

    • Asia Park / Sun Wheel: permanently closed (Google; Tuoi Tre).
    • Han River cruise on a weeknight: no fire show; cruises rate 3.9–4.0. Saturday 21:00 only.
    • Naman Retreat: recent Booking 8.3, "signs of age"; Google stars mostly staff-prompted.
    • Hair of the Dog bar, Hoi An: 5 of 30 recent reviews 1–3★ (theft, harassment).
    • Quán Hải Sản Bé Nữ: unpriced seafood ("shrimp 1,200,000 VND").
    • Hoi An beaches: seawall work; Da Nang ordered an emergency 2 km embankment in Apr 2026.
